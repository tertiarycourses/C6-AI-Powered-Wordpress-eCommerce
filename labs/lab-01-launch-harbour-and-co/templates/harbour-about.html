We make clothes for the Singapore commute

Harbour & Co started in 2019 in a shared studio at Ayer Rajah. Founder Rachel Lim spent eight years in buying for regional retailers before she set out to solve one problem: clothes that look right in a 20°C office and still breathe at 32°C outside.

How we choose a fabric

  • Every sample is worn on a real commute — MRT, hawker centre, meeting room — before it is approved.
  • We list fibre, weight in gsm and care on every product page, so you can compare like for like.
  • If a piece wrinkles badly or traps heat, it does not make the collection.
